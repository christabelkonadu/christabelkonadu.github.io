About

Biography

Christabel Konadu is a graduate student in Statistics at Western Michigan University, where she is a Graduate Assistant in the Department of Statistics. Her research focuses on statistical methods for clinical and public health data, with interests in survival and longitudinal data analysis, mixed-effects modeling, and machine learning for disease detection and prediction. She is particularly interested in the socioeconomic and public health determinants of chronic disease.

Christabel's training began in actuarial science at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST) in Ghana, where she built a foundation in risk, time series, and econometric modeling and gained industry experience in banking, insurance, and financial services. She has taught and mentored students in probability, statistical inference, and regression at KNUST and WMU.
